# Demand scan — 5 November 2026
Requested window: approximately 6 October–5 November 2026. This scan identifies dated marketplace signals; it does not establish a universal ranking of the world's highest-demand products or exact trailing-30-day demand for every service.

| Category | Source / measurement | Finding | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| AI video services | Upwork report published 14 Oct 2026; September marketplace searches | AI video was its most-searched AI-related term in September | Relevant CDM capability, but a video sales package would need portfolio and specific scope |
| AI workflow automation / AI software implementation | Same report; some growth comparisons are year-over-year | Workflow automation was second-fastest-growing AI-related search YoY; implementation/integration contract growth continued | Strong category signal, but integrations add discovery and access time |
| Beauty products | TokConnect, 30 days to 29 Oct 2026, US TikTok Shop estimated revenue | Medicube Smooth & Clear Body Care Set ranked first at estimated $6.46M | Inventory, product rights and fulfilment make this a poor fit for a 24-hour CDM sale |
| Press-on nails | Same estimated ranking | Second at estimated $3.39M | Existing sales elsewhere do not provide CDM stock or distribution |
| Blankets / home comfort | Same ranking | Comfrt Dreamer Blanket third at estimated $2.36M | Not selected for the immediate sprint |
| Website / enquiry-page service | CDM's live service catalogue plus a genuine existing prospect request reviewed privately | A buyer has already expressed redesign interest | Selected for speed to a legitimate customer, not claimed to be the top global trend |

## Commercial decision
Use the user's explicit fallback to sell an existing service immediately. Create original reusable layouts and a small paid scope. Fulfil the complimentary concept already promised to the warm school prospect, then offer a US$300 first build with a US$150 deposit after scope agreement. Existing service prospects receive relevant page examples rather than unrelated book promotions.

## Success definition
A prospect reply is a reply. Scope acceptance is a customer commitment. A verified deposit/order is a paid sale. None is interchangeable with a sent email or a published page.
